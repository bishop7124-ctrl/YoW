Help: what do these words mean?
Development

Work inside the product’s code, database, tests, or release system. You do not do this.

QA / testing

Trying the product and recording what happened. A failed test is useful information, not your mistake.

Production

The real customer website. Use disposable accounts when instructed.

Gate

A group of checks that must pass before taking money.

Evidence

A screenshot, date, or short note proving what happened. Never paste secrets.

Blocked

You cannot continue because something is missing. Mark it blocked and ask development.
