Hi everyone!

Well, it’s officially happening. Your Own World is out of beta and has officially launched! 🎉

First of all, I just want to say a massive thank you.

Whether you’ve been using YOW regularly, exploring the features, or simply signed up to see what it’s all about, I genuinely appreciate you being here.

Building Your Own World has been a huge project, and seeing people sign up and start creating their own worlds has been incredibly exciting. Your feedback, suggestions, and support throughout beta have helped shape the platform into what it is today.

So, what happens now?
With the official launch comes our new subscription options, designed to give you flexibility depending on how you use YOW.

🌱 Free – £0
Perfect for getting started! Keep building your world with access to a selection of YOW’s features, including one project and 5MB of storage.
 
✨ Monthly – £10/month
Unlock the full YOW experience with access to all premium features, without a long-term commitment.
 
🌟 Lifetime – £75 one-time payment
Prefer to pay once and get on with creating? Pay once and the downloadable Your Own World app is yours forever, with all the premium features and no monthly subscription. Cloud hosting covers using Your Own World online (in your browser, with sync and backups). Your first year is included, then it’s a small £6 a year to keep it going. If you ever choose not to, the app keeps working on your own computer.
 
🏅 Founders Lifetime – £50 one-time payment (first 100 only)
As a thank you to our earliest supporters, the first 100 Lifetime memberships are Founder memberships at just £50, with two years of cloud hosting included. That’s £25 off the standard Lifetime price, and once all 100 are claimed, they’re gone.

You can explore exactly what’s included with each option on our What’s Included page.

⏳ You’ve still got 30 days of beta access!
I don’t want anyone to feel rushed into making a decision, so all existing beta users will keep their current access until 2 December 2026.

After that, accounts without an active subscription or Lifetime membership will automatically move to the Free plan.

Don’t worry: your existing projects and creations won’t be deleted. However, some premium features will become unavailable until you upgrade.

This gives you plenty of time to explore the platform, see what works for you, and decide whether upgrading makes sense.

If you’ve got suggestions or questions, or just want to share what you’re working on, reply to this email or get in touch at support@yourownworld.co.uk.

Thank you again for being part of YOW’s journey so far. It really does mean a lot.
