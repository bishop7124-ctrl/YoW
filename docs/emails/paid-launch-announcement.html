Your Own World

Hi everyone!

I wanted to share a little update because something pretty exciting (and honestly, slightly terrifying!) is happening next week.

On Monday, 2 November, Your Own World is officially launching its paid plans! 🎉

When I first started building Your Own World, I really just wanted to create something that worked for me. As someone who loves writing and worldbuilding, I was tired of having characters, locations, timelines, and random ideas scattered across a million different places.

So I decided to build something myself.

What started as a personal project has turned into something much bigger than I ever expected. There are now over 200 people signed up, which still feels a little surreal considering this whole thing started as an idea I had for organising my own stories.

And now, after months of building, breaking things, fixing them, and probably adding far too many features, I’m finally ready to take the next step.

So, what’s happening on Monday?
Your Own World will be introducing two paid options:

Lifetime Access – £75
Pay once and the downloadable Your Own World app is yours forever, with all the premium features and no monthly subscription. Cloud hosting is the part that covers using Your Own World online (in your browser, with sync and backups). Your first year is included, then it’s a small £6 a year to keep it going. If you ever choose not to, the app keeps working for you on your own computer.
 
Monthly Subscription – £10/month
Access the premium features online without committing to a lifetime purchase.

And because I want to say thank you to the people who’ve been here early on, the first 100 Lifetime purchases will be just £50, with two years of cloud hosting included!

Don’t worry, though. Your Own World will still have a free plan. I never wanted this to become something where you have to pay just to start creating.

One thing to know: the beta access you have now will carry on for 30 days after launch, until Wednesday 2 December. After that your account moves to the free plan unless you choose a paid one, so please take a backup of anything precious while you’re in there.

I also want to be completely transparent: Your Own World is still growing. I’m a solo developer, and while I’ve put an enormous amount of time and love into building it, there are still things I want to improve, features I want to add, and probably a few bugs I haven’t found yet!

That’s where I’d love your help. What do you love? What’s confusing? What do you wish was there? Even a couple of lines would mean the world, and there’s still time for it to shape the launch.

Send me your feedback

But I genuinely believe in what I’m building, and I’m really excited to see where it goes.

Whether you’ve been actively using Your Own World, signed up to have a look around, or simply liked the idea enough to give it a chance, thank you. It really does mean a lot.

I’ll be sharing more details when everything goes live on Monday!

Happy worldbuilding,
Morgan
