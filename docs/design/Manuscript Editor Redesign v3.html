What changed, and why

Sixteen decisions across the four rounds.

  1. Top bar has three zones instead of twelve buttons.Where you are (left), how you're doing (centre), what you can do (right). Focus is the only filled button; Search, Pacing, Template, Import, Export, Finalise and version history moved into one overflow menu.
  2. A persistent, collapsible left rail.Acts, chapters and scenes with word counts and status squares. Collapsed it becomes a spine of ticks that still shows your position in the draft.
  3. The breadcrumb answers "where am I".Act · Chapter · Scene updates as you scroll or select, and doubles as the jump-to-scene control.
  4. One level of navigation on the right.The icon rail plus panel is now a single inspector with four tabs — Scene, Notes, Format, Progress — toggled by one button.
  5. Scene chrome is one quiet line.Number, title, status and word count on a single baseline above a hairline. POV, location and summary live in the inspector instead of interrupting the prose; notes become superscript marks in the text.
  6. Progress reads as one sentence.Words today against the goal in a single bar, streak and average as small text, one seven-day sparkline, three targets in a list. The three-stat grid and target accordions are gone.
  7. Adding structure is where the structure is.Hover between scenes in the rail for an inline +, or use the three explicit buttons at its foot. No modal to add a chapter.
  8. Mobile is one bar, not a rail plus tabs.Outline, Write, Inspector and AI; the rail slides in as a sheet, the inspector as a bottom sheet.
  9. Search, version history, finalise and AI share one surface.One full-height panel on the right, one at a time, at a fixed width; it overlays the inspector and reopens whatever you had last (⌘J). Four modals become one place.
  10. A narrower measure with a note gutter.Prose sits left-set at roughly 58 characters; notes live in the margin beside the line they belong to, with their bodies in the inspector.
  11. Notes start from the writing, not a panel.Select prose and a small bar offers Note or Ask AI; the note appears in the gutter and the inspector at once.
  12. Destructive actions are immediate, with one undo.Replace-all, restoring a snapshot and deleting a scene all confirm afterwards in a single toast rather than asking first.
  13. A finalised draft is a reading mode, not a modal.Finalise prepares and exports inside the surface; “Open reader” gives the frozen copy the whole screen, and Esc returns you to the draft.
  14. Three modes instead of one crowded surface.Write is bare: no inspector, no gutter, no note marks, no metadata beyond a faint scene name, rail collapsed to its spine. Edit is the full apparatus — notes, scene fields, format, progress, search, AI. Finalised is read-only.
  15. Notes are an editing tool.Note marks, the gutter and the “Note” action only exist in Edit; Write mode's selection bar offers formatting alone.
  16. Finalised reads two ways.A continuous manuscript flow, or a book spread — two justified pages, running page numbers, arrow navigation — with export beside the view switch. Esc returns you to Edit.
