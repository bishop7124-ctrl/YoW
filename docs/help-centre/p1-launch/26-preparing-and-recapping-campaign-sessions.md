# Preparing and recapping campaign sessions

Campaign projects add session-focused planning fields to the writing workspace so preparation and outcomes can stay with each session.

## The how

1. Open a D&D or Tabletop Campaign project.
2. Open the campaign writing workspace and focus a session.
3. In the session details, complete the preparation fields you need, such as intended encounters, participants, goals or notes.
4. Write or update the session content.
5. After play, complete the recap or outcome fields.
6. Update the session status and confirm the **Saved** indicator.

Use linked characters, locations and catalogue records to open the supporting project material while preparing.

## The why: plan and outcome belong together

Campaign structure uses arcs, sessions and encounters rather than prose-only terminology. Preparation records intent; recap records what actually happened. Keeping both makes later sessions easier to plan when players depart from the expected path.

These fields support the campaign workflow but do not roll dice, resolve game rules or automatically change character sheets. D&D and system-neutral campaigns share the broad structure, while their defaults and Party tools may differ.

Session targets count planned session structure rather than manuscript word count. A recap can still contain ordinary prose and contribute to saved content.
