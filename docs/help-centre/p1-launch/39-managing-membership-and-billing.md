# Managing membership and billing

The Membership panel shows the account’s access, renewal information and any available billing controls.

## The how

1. Open **Account Settings → Membership**.
2. Review the plan, access level, trial or hosting dates and active-project information.
3. Use the displayed plan control to register interest, start checkout or change access, depending on what is currently available.
4. Existing billed accounts can use **Manage subscription & billing** or **Billing history & receipts** where shown.
5. Complete billing changes in the secure billing page, then return to YOW and refresh if the membership does not update immediately.

Before cancelling or moving to Free, export restore-ready backups and choose the project you want to remain editable.

## The why: billing and access are connected but distinct

Monthly subscription cancellation, one-time purchase receipts and Cloud Mode renewal can expose different controls. A billed change may take a short time to reach YOW after the external billing page completes.

Moving to Free makes one selected project editable and retains other projects as view-only and exportable; it does not transfer ownership of the writing. Lifetime desktop access and optional Cloud Mode can have separate statuses.

At the time this article pack was prepared, public paid-plan buttons may still register interest rather than complete a purchase. Re-check the live wording and checkout availability before publication.
