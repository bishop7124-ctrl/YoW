# Planning a character journey

The character journey records how one character changes and the story moments that earn that change.

## The how

1. Open **Characters** and select a character.
2. Open the character’s **Journey** area.
3. Select **Begin the journey** or **Edit overview**.
4. Choose an **Arc type** and whether the journey covers **This project** or **Across the series**.
5. Complete the beginning, ending and journey-note fields you need, then select **Save journey**.
6. Under **Journey beats**, select **Add beat**.
7. Add a title, story phase, description and optional timeline, character, chapter or scene links.
8. Use the arrow buttons to reorder beats; use **Edit** or **Delete** to maintain them.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open a fictional character’s Journey with an overview and three beats. Show **Before & after**, a major **Turning point**, reorder arrows and **Add beat**. Use links to disposable scenes or timeline events.

## The why: change supported by evidence

The overview describes the character’s broad movement, while beats connect that intention to specific choices and turning points. A beat can record what happens, what changes internally and why it matters without adding those notes to manuscript prose.

**Across the series** describes the intended scope; actual cross-project visibility depends on the project’s series and sharing settings. Story links are optional and remain separate records, so deleting a beat does not delete the linked scene or timeline event.

Reordering changes the journey sequence only. It does not reorder manuscript chapters or timeline dates.
