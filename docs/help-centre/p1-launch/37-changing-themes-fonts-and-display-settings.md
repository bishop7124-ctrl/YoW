# Changing themes, fonts and display settings

Appearance settings change YOW’s interface colours, typography and atmosphere across the account.

## The how

1. Open **Account Settings → Appearance**.
2. Choose a built-in theme under **Automatic**, **Light & dark** or **Alternatives**.
3. Under **Custom colours**, adjust Canvas, Surface, Ink, Muted ink, Action and Line.
4. Tune **Corner roundness** and **Atmosphere strength**.
5. Choose a **Font family**, including the dyslexia-friendly option where available.
6. Enter a name under **Save current as preset** and select **Save** if you want to keep the combination.
7. Reorder or delete personal presets with their controls.
8. Save the settings to the account.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open **Account Settings → Appearance**. Show the live preview, theme groups, custom colour controls, tuning sliders, font choices and one personal preset. Use no personal data.

## The why: account interface versus manuscript format

Appearance changes YOW’s application chrome and general typography. Manuscript **Inspector → Format** separately controls the writing page’s typeface, size, spacing, alignment and paragraph presentation.

**Automatic** follows the supported system preference. Custom colours should retain readable contrast between Ink and Canvas, and between Action and surrounding surfaces. Personal presets preserve a complete look so you can experiment without losing a preferred combination.

Preference cookies or account syncing may be required for settings to follow you between sessions and devices.
