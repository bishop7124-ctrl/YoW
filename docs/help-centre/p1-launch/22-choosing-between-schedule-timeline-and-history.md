# Choosing between Schedule, Timeline and History

Schedule, Timeline and History all use dates, but they answer different planning questions.

## The how

Choose **Schedule** when you need:

- A month grid or chronological task list
- Durations that can be dragged or resized
- A custom month and weekday system
- Planned sessions, deadlines or appointments

Choose **Timeline** when you need:

- Ordered story or world events
- Dates or ranges, eras, tags and categories
- Direct links to involved characters and locations

Choose **History** when you need:

- Longer chronicle entries
- Background events and eras
- Context that may sit outside the manuscript’s immediate sequence

## The why: avoid duplicate sources of truth

A campaign session planned for next month belongs naturally in Schedule. The battle that occurs in the story belongs in Timeline. The century of conflict leading to it belongs in History.

The areas can reference the same people and places, but they do not automatically mirror every change across one another. Avoid creating identical records in all three unless each serves a distinct purpose. Use consistent date language when your project has a custom calendar.

When uncertain, start with the question you need the screen to answer: “When do I need to do this?”, “What happens in sequence?” or “What happened in this world?”.
