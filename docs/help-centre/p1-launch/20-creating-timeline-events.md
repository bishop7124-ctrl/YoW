# Creating timeline events

Timeline records story or world events, including date ranges, eras and links to the people and places involved.

## The how

1. Open **Timeline**.
2. Select **New Event**.
3. Add a title, date or date range and description.
4. Choose an era and category where applicable.
5. Add tags and link characters or locations.
6. Save the event.
7. Use era chips and **Search events** to filter the timeline.
8. Select an event to review its details, then choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

## The why: ordered events and narrative evidence

Timeline is suited to events whose sequence matters to the story or world. Dates may follow your fictional convention; they are displayed as entered rather than forced into a real-world calendar.

Eras offer a broad grouping and filters offer a narrower view. Character and location links make it possible to move from chronology to the relevant project records without duplicating their profiles.

Deleting an event removes the timeline record but keeps linked characters and locations. If an event belongs mainly to background chronicle rather than plot sequence, **History** may be a better home.
