# Recording world history and managing eras

History stores chronicle entries and eras for background events, cultures and long periods that shape the project world.

## The how

### Add a history entry

1. Open **History**.
2. Select **New**.
3. Add a title, date or date range, era and chronicle text.
4. Link relevant characters or locations.
5. Save the entry.

### Manage eras

1. Select **Eras**.
2. Add or edit an era and its date range.
3. Save your changes.
4. Delete an era only after reviewing the affected entries.

Use **Search** to filter entries. Select an entry to read it, then use **Edit** or **Delete**.

## The why: a chronicle rather than a plot board

History is designed for context: dynasties, wars, migrations, discoveries and other records that may precede or surround the manuscript. Timeline is usually better for a sequence you actively track through the story.

Eras group entries but are separate records. Removing an era does not mean deleting the underlying history; review how affected entries will appear without that grouping. Entries marked as originating from Timeline may be shown as **Timeline only** and should be edited in their source area.

Dates are descriptive and can use world-specific wording. Keep a consistent convention if you want searching and reading order to remain clear.
