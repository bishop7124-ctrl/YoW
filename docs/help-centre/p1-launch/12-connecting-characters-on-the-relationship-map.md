# Connecting characters on the Relationship Map

The Relationship Map shows directional connections between saved characters and lets you focus on one person at a time.

## The how

1. Open **Characters → Relationship Map**.
2. Choose a **Focus character** or select a character on the canvas.
3. Under **Add a connection**, choose another character and a relationship type.
4. Select **Add Connection**.
5. Use **×** beside a non-family link to remove it.
6. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use **−**, **+** and **Fit all**.
7. Select **Open Character Profile** to view the focused record.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open a disposable project with five connected characters. Focus one character and show the canvas, **Add a connection**, connection list, zoom controls and **Fit all**. Include at least one directional non-family link.

## The why: direction and family ownership

Connections are directional. “A mentors B” is not automatically the same as “B mentors A”, and YOW can retain different types in each direction. The count on a character represents unique connected people rather than the number of individual link facts.

Family relationships are managed in **Family Tree** and appear read-only on the Relationship Map. This prevents two screens from creating contradictory parent, partner or sibling information.

Focusing filters the visual emphasis; it does not delete characters outside the immediate network. Dense maps are easier to read with **Fit all**, zoom and pan.
