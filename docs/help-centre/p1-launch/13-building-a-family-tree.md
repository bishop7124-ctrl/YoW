# Building a Family Tree

The Family Tree records parent, partner, child, sibling, guardian and extended-family relationships between project characters.

## The how

1. Open **Characters → Family Tree**.
2. Choose a **Focus character** and a **View** such as **Direct lineage**, **Immediate family**, **Extended family** or **Full dynasty**.
3. Select a character in the chart.
4. Under **Add Relative**, choose the relationship and another saved character.
5. Open **Details** to add family type, status, dates or notes.
6. Save the connection.
7. Use the relationship lists in **Family Details** to review or remove links.

Use the adoption filter when you want adoptive connections included or hidden in the chart.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open a fictional three-generation family and focus the middle generation. Show the **View** selector, adoption control, chart and **Family Details → Add Relative** panel. Use non-personal names.

## The why: one source for family facts

Family links live here and are read-only in the Relationship Map. YOW derives reciprocal meaning where appropriate—for example, adding a parent establishes the corresponding child relationship—so review the selected role before saving.

Dates and status can describe marriages, separations, guardianships or other time-bounded connections. They are descriptive fields and do not automatically create timeline or history entries.

View filters change what is drawn, not what is stored. **Unlinked Characters** groups saved characters that have no family connection in the current project.
