# Reviewing series continuity

The Series dashboard brings possible matches, warnings, journeys, timelines and map references together for cross-project continuity review.

## The how

1. Open a series from the **Library**.
2. Open its continuity area.
3. Review the index of shared or similarly named records.
4. Open **Possible matches** and compare source projects before deciding whether records refer to the same thing.
5. Review continuity warnings and mark a warning reviewed only after checking its evidence.
6. Inspect character journey lanes and the combined timeline for changes across story order.
7. Select a map preview or source link to open the original project record.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open a three-project disposable series with one possible character match, one warning, a journey lane, combined timeline and map preview. Show project/source labels and the reviewed-warning control.

## The why: evidence across ordered projects

Continuity tools compare saved records and series order. Similar names can be unrelated, and different names can refer to the same entity, so YOW presents possible matches rather than silently combining them.

Marking a warning reviewed records your decision; it does not rewrite the source record. Open the source project to make a correction. Forward-sharing and **Include later works** settings affect what a project can see.

The dashboard is an index, not a downloadable Series Bible. That export is not currently implemented.
