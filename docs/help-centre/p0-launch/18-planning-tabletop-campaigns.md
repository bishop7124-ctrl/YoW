# Planning D&D and system-neutral tabletop campaigns

Campaign projects organise preparation into arcs, sessions and encounters while keeping NPCs, factions, maps, lore and history connected.

## The how

### Create a campaign

1. In the Library, select **+ Project**.
2. Choose **D&D Campaign** or **Tabletop Campaign**.
3. Enter a title and optional description.
4. Select **Create**.
5. Open **Sessions** and build the starter arc, session and encounter structure.
6. Set a session target in **Project Settings** if wanted.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open **New Project** with the two campaign cards visible. Show their descriptions and highlight the selected type. Use a disposable campaign title such as “Ashfall Company”.

### Prepare an encounter

1. Open **Sessions**.
2. Select an encounter in the manuscript rail.
3. Add encounter text and use the **Prep** fields for goals, participants and other available preparation details.
4. Add POV, location or summary information in **Inspector** where useful.
5. Wait for **Saved**.

### Record the outcome

1. Return to the encounter after play.
2. Complete its **Recap** fields.
3. Update the encounter status.
4. Create or update linked Characters, Locations, Lore, Timeline and History records as the campaign changes.

## The why: D&D and Tabletop Campaign

**D&D Campaign** uses D&D-flavoured fantasy language and story indicators. **Tabletop Campaign** is system-neutral and avoids assuming a specific ruleset or genre.

Both provide:

- Arcs, sessions and encounters
- The **Party** room with character sheets and dice roller
- Campaign-ready Factions, Locations, Lore, Schedule, Timeline, History and Maps
- Session targets instead of word targets
- Campaign-aware AI context on plans that include AI

YOW is a campaign bible and writing workspace, not a rules engine or virtual tabletop. It does not replace the rules, initiative, combat map or character service used by your game group.
