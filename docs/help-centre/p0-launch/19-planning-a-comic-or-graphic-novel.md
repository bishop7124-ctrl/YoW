# Planning a Comic or Graphic Novel

Comic projects replace the prose manuscript with a sequential-art planner built around volumes, issues, pages and panels.

## The how

### Create the structure

1. Create a **Comic / Graphic Novel** project.
2. Open **Pages**.
3. Select **+ Volume**.
4. Add an issue inside the volume.
5. Open the issue and select **+ Page**.
6. Select **+ Add panel** on the page.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open a disposable Comic project with one volume, one issue, two pages and several panels. Show the structure sidebar and an expanded page. Use generic sample titles and fictional content.

### Plan a page

1. Enter an optional page title and summary.
2. Choose its page type, production status and page-turn setting.
3. Add visual direction, production notes and characters on the page.
4. Add panels and reorder them as needed.

### Plan a panel

1. Expand the panel.
2. Choose its shot type, layout and status.
3. Describe what the reader sees.
4. Add art notes and characters.
5. Add dialogue balloons, captions and sound effects.
6. Record continuity notes.

You can move panels up or down, duplicate pages, and attach a PDF or image reference where the interface offers the reference control.

## The why: comic-specific planning

Comic projects treat a page as the main manuscript unit and panels as its internal sequence. They therefore do not use the prose editor’s Writing, Editing, Review and Finalised modes.

The planner separates reader-facing script content from visual and production direction:

- Description records the visible action.
- Art notes hold composition, expression, props and motion guidance.
- Dialogue, captions and sound effects remain distinct.
- Continuity notes track costume, props, time and other cross-panel details.

Deleting a volume removes its issues and pages; deleting an issue removes its pages; deleting a page removes its panels. Export or back up the project before a broad deletion.
