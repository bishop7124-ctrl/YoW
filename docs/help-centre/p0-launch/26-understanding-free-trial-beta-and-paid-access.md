# Understanding Free, trial, Beta and paid access

Your YOW membership controls project count, cloud storage, AI access and desktop entitlement; it does not change ownership of your writing.

## The how

1. Open the account menu and select **Account Settings**.
2. Select **Membership**.
3. Review the plan badge, access status, storage allowance and any trial, Beta or cloud-hosting notice.
4. Use the available membership or billing control if you need to register interest, manage billing, renew Cloud Mode or change plan.

📸 SCREENSHOT: Open **Account Settings → Membership** with a dedicated documentation account. Show the plan badge, status rows, storage information and available plan controls. Crop out the email address and any billing identifiers.

## The why: what each access type includes

### Free

- One fully editable project
- Core manuscript and worldbuilding tools, including Map Builder
- 250 MB of cloud storage
- DOCX, PDF and ZIP export
- No AI Tools

Other retained projects are view-only and exportable after a downgrade. Choosing the active Free project does not delete them.

### Trial

A trial temporarily provides broader access and an 8 GB storage allowance. The Membership tab shows the end date and days remaining.

### Beta Tester

Eligible Beta accounts receive full temporary access, unlimited projects, AI Tools and a 15 GB allowance during the applicable Beta period. Any transition notice appears in Membership.

### Monthly, Lifetime and Founder

The implemented plan definitions provide unlimited projects and broader access, with different desktop and Cloud Mode entitlements. Founder includes a 15 GB allowance; Monthly and Lifetime use 8 GB in the current rules.

At the time this draft was prepared, public paid-plan buttons register interest rather than completing a purchase. Re-check this paragraph and the live buttons before publication at paid launch.

## Plan changes and your work

Moving to Free does not transfer ownership or automatically erase projects. YOW asks you to choose one editable project; other retained projects become view-only and remain exportable. AI Tools lock until eligible access returns.

Lifetime cloud hosting can lapse independently of the desktop licence. In that state, the desktop remains usable in Local Mode while web cloud access follows Free limits until Cloud Mode is renewed.

Always export restore-ready backups before cancelling, downgrading or deleting an account.
