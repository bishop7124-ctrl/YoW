# Backing up and deleting your account

Account deletion permanently removes the account and its associated YOW data, so YOW provides project-backup controls before the final confirmation.

## The how

### Download backups first

1. Open **Account Settings → Profile**.
2. Find **Delete account** under **Danger zone**.
3. Select **Delete account…**.
4. Download individual project backups or select the control to download all backups.
5. Open the downloaded archive and verify every expected project before continuing.

### Delete the account

1. Continue from the deletion dialog only after verifying the backups.
2. Review the permanent-deletion warning.
3. Continue to the final confirmation step.
4. Type `delete my account` exactly as shown.
5. Select **Delete my account**.
6. Wait until YOW confirms the account is gone and signs you out.

## The why: what is deleted

Deletion is intended to remove the authentication account and associated projects, scenes, characters, locations, lore, media and account records. It is not the same as signing out, cancelling a plan or deleting one project.

The final typed phrase reduces accidental deletion but does not provide a recovery period. A verified external backup is the recovery path you control.

### Safe backup checklist

- Use **Export all as ZIP backups** for structured restoration.
- Confirm the outer ZIP opens.
- Confirm one folder exists for each project.
- Open representative inner backup ZIPs.
- Keep the archive somewhere outside YOW and outside the desktop vault.

Do not delete an account to solve a login, storage or billing problem. Use password recovery, remove unneeded media, change plan or contact support first.
