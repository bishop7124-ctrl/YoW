# Creating your YOW account and signing in

YOW accounts let you return to your projects and, in Cloud Mode, keep saved project data available across your signed-in devices.

## The how

### Create an account with email

1. Open the YOW homepage and select **Get started free**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Create a password containing at least eight characters, an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
4. Select **Create Account**.
5. If **Check your inbox** appears, open YOW's confirmation email and follow its link.
6. Return to YOW and sign in.

### Create or sign in with Google

1. Open the sign-up or login page.
2. Select **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account you want to connect to YOW.
4. Complete Google's sign-in screen; YOW will return you to your workspace.

### Sign in with email

1. Select **Log in** on the YOW homepage.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign In**.

Use the eye button in the password field to show or hide the entered password.

### Sign out

1. Open the account menu in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Sign Out**.

## The why: understanding your account

Your account identifies the projects, membership and account-saved preferences that belong to you. A new user is offered the welcome flow; a returning user opens their Library.

Email sign-up may require confirmation before the account becomes active. From the **Check your inbox** screen you can select **Resend email**, **Go to login**, or **Try again** with another address.

Use the same Google account each time you choose Google sign-in. A different Google account may open a separate YOW Library.

The web app signs out after 24 hours without activity. Sign in again to continue; this does not delete any projects. The desktop app does not use this browser inactivity timeout.

If YOW reports that the email already has an account, use **Sign in** or **Forgot password?** instead. Never send a password, confirmation link or reset link to support.
