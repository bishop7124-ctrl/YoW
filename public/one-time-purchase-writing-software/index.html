Buy once

Writing and worldbuilding software you can buy once.

Your Own World combines a manuscript editor with characters, family trees, lore, maps and timelines. Lifetime is a single payment of £75, or £50 for the first 100 customers, with no subscription needed to keep using the app. A Free plan lets you try it first.

What the one-time Lifetime payment covers

Lifetime is permanent access to the YOW application. It is separate from the optional hosted Cloud service.

  • Price: £75 once, or £50 once for the first 100 Lifetime customers, who also receive permanent Founder status.
  • Unlimited projects and the full writing and worldbuilding toolkit.
  • Permanent desktop and offline access for Windows and macOS.
  • YOW Cloud included for one year (two years for the first 100 customers). After that Cloud is optional at £6 per year.
  • If you skip the Cloud renewal: your licence stays active and you keep working locally and offline. Cloud sync and hosted storage fall back to the Free limits.

Lifetime does not promise a particular roadmap or an indefinite stream of new features. Buy it for what it does today.

What you get for the price

Manuscript

Write in acts or parts, chapters and scenes, with word targets and progress tracking. See novel writing software.

Characters and family trees

Character dossiers and relationship maps, plus family trees that cover spouses, adoptive and step relationships, half-siblings and heirs. See character management.

Lore, maps and timelines

A linked lore encyclopedia, a map builder and a timeline with an in-world calendar. See worldbuilding software.

Things to know before you buy

  • The desktop app is not yet signed or notarized by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer shows a one-time warning on first launch. The download page explains the steps.
  • YOW is designed for laptop and desktop use. Phone and tablet use is not officially supported.
  • AI features are optional, need a paid plan, and use your own OpenRouter or OpenAI API key, so you pay that provider directly. See the AI overview.
  • All sales are final except where the law gives you a right to a refund. The Free plan (one project, 250 MB) is there so you can test before paying.
  • If you prefer to pay month to month, Monthly is £10 per month and can be cancelled any time. It does not include the desktop app.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a subscription for Your Own World?

Only if you choose Monthly. Lifetime is a one-time payment, and the Cloud service after the included period is optional.

Can I use it offline?

Yes, with the desktop app that comes with Lifetime.

Can I export my work?

Yes. All plans can export DOCX, PDF and a restorable project ZIP.
