Character Management

Keep every character consistent, from first draft to final book.

Your Own World gives writers a character dossier for every person in their story, plus relationship maps, family trees and factions. Each one is linked to your lore, timeline and manuscript, so a character's details live in one place.

What goes wrong without a character tracker

Eye colours drift, ages stop adding up, and a minor character from book one quietly changes personality by book three. Keeping character facts in scattered documents makes these slips hard to catch.

Character dossiers

A profile for each character covering backstory, motivations, voice, appearance, arc and relationships, so you can check a detail before you write the scene.

Relationship maps

Map how characters relate to one another and see alliances, rivalries and secrets at a glance. See also the family tree builder for bloodlines and dynasties.

Factions and organisations

Group characters into guilds, houses, armies or governments, with custom faction logos. Useful for politics-heavy fantasy and for tabletop campaigns.

Characters that connect to the rest of your project

A character sheet on its own is a note. In Your Own World it is linked to the rest of your workspace.

  • Characters can be linked from your manuscript, and a linked entry opens beside the page you are writing.
  • Their timeline events and lore entries stay attached to them.
  • Factions and family trees draw from the same character records.
  • Series management groups related books together, so a multi-book story stays organised in one place.
  • It works for novels, novellas, short stories, comic/graphic novels and tabletop campaigns, with 5E character tools for fifth-edition campaigns.
