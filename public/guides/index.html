Guides

Writing and worldbuilding guides

Practical, tool-neutral advice for fiction writers, with notes on how to do each step in Your Own World.

How to organise a fantasy world

Start with what your story touches. Record the people, places, history and rules that appear on the page, link them to each other, and add depth only when a scene needs it.

How to choose worldbuilding software

Decide what you need to track, then check each tool for those features, how it charges, and whether you can export your work. Test with a free plan or trial before paying.

How to track characters across a book series

Keep one record per character, record facts as they become canon, and note what changes between books so you can check each new scene against it.

How to build a fictional family tree

Start with one anchor character, work outward by generation, record relationship types explicitly, and check ages against your timeline.

From idea to manuscript: a novel planning workflow

Capture the idea, write a one-paragraph premise, outline in beats, build only the world the story needs, then draft scene by scene and revise.
