# The Hollow Crown - D&D 5E Campaign

A level 3-8 campaign for a party of four. Setting: the frontier kingdom of Varnholt. Tone: heroic with political intrigue.

## Premise

The old king's crown has been stolen from his tomb. Three factions each claim the right to find it first, and the stolen crown is slowly turning Varnholt's undead restless.

## Factions

- **The Ashen Concord** - noble houses who want the crown to legitimise a new king.
- **The Grave Wardens** - monastic order sworn to keep the dead at rest.
- **The Redhand Company** - mercenaries hired by an unknown patron.

## Non-Player Characters

- **Lady Seraphine Vance** - leader of the Ashen Concord, charming and ruthless. Wants a puppet king.
- **Brother Anselm** - Grave Warden elder, kind, quietly dying, knows the crown's true purpose.
- **Kessa Redhand** - mercenary captain, honourable by her own code; her patron is the real villain.
- **The Hollow King** - undead monarch, the campaign's final antagonist.

## Locations

- **Varnholt Keep** - the capital, where the Concord holds court.
- **The Weeping Barrows** - the royal burial ground, now haunted.
- **Gallowmere Marsh** - lawless wetland where the Redhand Company camps.

## Session Prep: Session 1 - The Empty Tomb

**Goal:** the party discovers the theft and chooses whom to trust.

**Scenes:**
1. A messenger finds the party at the Gilded Stag inn with a sealed request from Lady Vance.
2. Investigation of the royal tomb in the Weeping Barrows: broken seals, muddy boot prints, a dropped Redhand badge.
3. Combat: four skeletons and a wight guarding the inner crypt.

**Encounter - Crypt of the Wight** (level 3): 1 wight (CR 3), 4 skeletons (CR 1/4). Terrain: cracked floor, two pillars for cover, a sarcophagus the wight rises from.

**Treasure:** 150 gp in grave goods, a silvered dagger, a torn map fragment of Gallowmere Marsh.

## Session Recap: Session 1

The party reached the Weeping Barrows at dusk, fought the wight, and recovered the map fragment. They chose to shadow Kessa Redhand rather than report to Lady Vance. Open thread: who is Kessa's patron?
