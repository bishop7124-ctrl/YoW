# Ashfall Station - Tabletop RPG Campaign

A system-neutral sci-fi horror campaign for 3-5 players. Setting: a mining station on a dead moon. Tone: tense, claustrophobic.

## Premise

The crew of Ashfall Station wakes from hypersleep eleven years early. The supply ship is gone, the corridors are dark, and something in the lower decks is answering the intercom.

## Factions

- **Ashfall Crew** - the player characters and the survivors.
- **The Concordia Mining Corp** - absentee owner; its orders keep arriving with impossible timestamps.
- **The Echo** - an unknown presence in the station's systems.

## Non-Player Characters

- **Dr. Imre Solace** - station physician, calm, hiding that she woke first.
- **Dock Foreman Pike** - gruff, loyal, wants to restart the reactor at any cost.
- **ARIA** - station AI, helpful, increasingly inconsistent.

## Locations

- **Habitat Ring** - living quarters, mostly intact.
- **Reactor Core** - cold, humming faintly.
- **Deep Shaft Nine** - sealed mining shaft; the intercom signal comes from here.

## Session Prep: Session 1 - Early Wake

**Goal:** establish dread, give the players a clear first objective (restore power to the Habitat Ring).

**Scenes:**
1. Waking in the cryo bay; frost on the glass, one pod already empty.
2. Exploring dark corridors; ARIA gives directions that do not match the map.
3. First sign of the Echo: the intercom says a crew member's name.

**Encounter - The Flooded Corridor:** a skill challenge (three successes before three failures) to cross a flooded service corridor with failing lights. A failure triggers a pressure-door malfunction.

## Session Recap: Session 1

The crew restored emergency power to the Habitat Ring and found Dr. Solace already awake. ARIA claimed no one had woken early. Open thread: why is one pod empty?
