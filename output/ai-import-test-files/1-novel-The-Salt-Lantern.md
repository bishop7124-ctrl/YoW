# The Salt Lantern

A novel in three parts. Genre: coastal fantasy mystery. Tone: quiet, atmospheric.

## Characters

- **Mara Venn** - protagonist, 29, lighthouse keeper's daughter turned salvage diver. Stubborn, dry humour, afraid of deep open water since her father vanished.
- **Tobias Quill** - harbour archivist, 41, careful and secretive; knows more about the lighthouse than he admits.
- **Captain Ilsa Brandt** - harbourmaster, 55, pragmatic, quietly protecting the town's smuggling income.
- **The Lantern Keeper** - a figure said to tend the old light at night; may be a ghost, may be a person.

## Locations

- **Saltmere** - fishing town built on stilts over a tidal flat.
- **Greywatch Lighthouse** - abandoned, its lamp burns without fuel on foggy nights.
- **The Drowned Quay** - the old harbour, visible only at the lowest tides.

## Lore

The Salt Lantern is a lamp made from sea-glass and fused salt. Legend says it guides drowned sailors home. Anyone who carries it into the water can hear the names of the dead.

## Timeline

- Year 0: Greywatch Lighthouse is built after the Great Wreck.
- Year 61: Mara's father, Edrin Venn, disappears on a foggy night.
- Year 90: Present day. The lamp lights itself for the first time in decades.

## Part One: The Light That Should Not Burn

### Chapter 1: Low Tide

Mara Venn waded across the tidal flats at dawn, boots sucking at the mud, a salvage net slung over one shoulder. Saltmere stood behind her on its tarred stilts, smoke rising from a dozen chimneys. She had promised herself she would not look at the lighthouse. She looked at the lighthouse.

Greywatch stood grey and broken on its spit of rock. Last night, for the first time in thirty years, its lamp had burned. Every fisherman in town had seen it. Nobody wanted to talk about it.

By the time she reached the Drowned Quay, the water had drawn back far enough to show the old stone steps. Something glinted at the foot of them: a lantern of green glass crusted white with salt. She knew it from her father's stories. She did not touch it. Not yet.

### Chapter 2: The Archivist

Tobias Quill kept the harbour archive in a room that smelled of damp paper and lamp oil. When Mara laid a rubbing of the lantern's base on his desk, his hands went very still.

"Where did you see this?" he asked.

"The Drowned Quay. Is it what I think it is?"

"It is what your father was looking for." He closed the ledger he had been writing in, too quickly. "Mara, if the Salt Lantern has surfaced, others will come for it. Captain Brandt will come for it first."

## Part Two: The Names of the Drowned

### Chapter 3: Brandt's Offer

Captain Ilsa Brandt received Mara on the harbour steps, as she received everyone, with a mug of black tea and no hurry. She offered a fair price for the lantern, no questions asked. Mara noticed that she had not asked what the lantern was.

### Chapter 4: Into the Fog

That night Mara rowed out alone. The fog closed over the boat like a lid. When she lifted the lantern, the green glass woke, and in the silence under the water she heard a voice say her father's name, and then her own.

## Part Three: What the Lamp Keeps

### Chapter 5: Greywatch

At the top of the lighthouse, the Lantern Keeper was waiting. It was not a ghost. It was Edrin Venn, older and thinner, who had been keeping the light alive so the drowned could find their way home. He asked his daughter one question: would she take the lamp, or let it go dark?
