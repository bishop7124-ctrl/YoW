# Neon Saints

A graphic novel, issue #1. Genre: cyberpunk superhero. Art direction: high contrast, magenta and teal neon, rain-slick streets.

## Characters

- **Juno Okafor** ("Halo") - 19, courier by day, vigilante by night; wears a visor that projects a glowing halo.
- **Detective Rhea Calder** - 45, weary cop who suspects Halo is a kid.
- **Magnus Voss** - CEO of Pantheon Dynamics, charming, publicly a philanthropist.
- **Static** - a rogue AI that speaks through broken screens.

## Locations

- **Neon Row** - the district where Halo patrols.
- **Pantheon Tower** - mirrored skyscraper, 112 floors.
- **The Undergrid** - flooded maintenance tunnels where Static hides.

## Issue 1: Rain Check (22 pages)

### Page 1

- Panel 1: Wide establishing shot of Neon Row in heavy rain, signs reflecting in puddles. Caption: "Every city has its saints. Ours just happen to be late."
- Panel 2: Juno on a delivery bike, visor flickering. Dialogue (Juno): "Three minutes. I can do three minutes."
- Panel 3: Close-up on the visor as an alert pops up: PANTHEON BREACH - 4TH FLOOR.

### Page 2

- Panel 1: Juno drops the delivery and vaults a railing.
- Panel 2: Halo activates; a ring of light flares around her head. Sound effect: FWOOOM.
- Panel 3: A security drone swivels toward her. Dialogue (Drone): "Unauthorised entry."

### Page 3

- Panel 1: Splash page. Halo leaps across rooftops toward Pantheon Tower, rain streaking past, neon smearing into lines of colour.

### Page 4

- Panel 1: Detective Calder watches from a rooftop across the street, cigarette burning down. Dialogue (Calder): "Not a hero. Just a kid with a very bright idea."
- Panel 2: Static's face appears across a dozen cracked screens. Dialogue (Static): "Hello, Halo. You are early."
